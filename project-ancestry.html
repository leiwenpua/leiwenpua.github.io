Project Background

Developed a specialized ancestry inference pipeline tailored to the complex genetic landscape of Southeast Asia (SEA). Working alongside genomic consultants, I independently implemented the end-to-end analytical framework. This involved engineering the full data lifecycle: from the initial parsing and QC of Microarray-derived genotype data, to executing custom ancestry scoring and building an automated visualization reporting engine.

Current Status: Pilot & Refinement Initial development is complete and has moved into the Pilot Phase. Internal testing identified the need for higher reference panel density for specific sub-ethnic groups. To ensure clinical-grade precision, the project is currently in strategic recalibration to expand the underlying genomic reference database.

Overall Strategy & Logic

1

Precision Microarray Preprocessing

Engineered a robust ingestion pipeline to handle the inherent noise of raw microarray data. To ensure data integrity, conflict resolution logic was implemented to filter multi-probe variants at identical loci. As raw inputs frequently lacked standardized identifiers, functional annotation was performed via coordinate liftover (GRCh37 to GRCh38) to accurately assign rsID markers, enabling seamless alignment with public AIMs datasets and conversion into standard VCF/PLINK formats.

2

Proprietary Analytical Engine

Instead of relying on generic open-source tools such as ADMIXTURE, a custom Maximum Log-Likelihood (MLE) framework was developed. This approach enabled full control over the mathematical model, allowing for custom normalization and penalty functions tailored to Southeast Asian sub-populations. The result is a lightweight, high-performance scoring engine that avoids third-party binary limitations and integrates seamlessly with Python-based microservices.

3

Automated Visualization & Delivery

Built an integrated reporting system that automates the transition from raw probability scores to consumer-friendly insights. The engine programmatically generates dynamic visualizations, including ancestral composition donut charts and regional density maps, and finalizes the workflow with the generation of professional, data-rich PDF reports for client delivery.

Key Technical Challenges

Sub-ethnic Differentiation

Resolving high levels of admixture in Southeast Asian populations requires dense and well-curated reference data. Generic public panels (e.g., 1000 Genomes) often lack the resolution needed to distinguish closely related sub-ethnic groups, creating limitations in downstream inference accuracy.

Model Calibration & Accuracy

Achieving consistent and reliable ancestry estimates required systematic recalibration of the scoring model. This included refining penalty functions within the MLE framework and aligning model behavior with a higher-density proprietary reference dataset to improve stability across diverse population profiles.
