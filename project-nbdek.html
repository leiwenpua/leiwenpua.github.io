NbDEK Structural Analysis

Homology Modelling Molecular Docking Protein Structure Validation Physicochemical Characterization

Project Background

This study was my Bachelor's Final Year Project, focusing on the in-silico characterization of NbDEK, a calpain-type cysteine protease from Nicotiana benthamiana.

At the time, the 3D structure of NbDEK was uncharacterized, creating a significant gap in understanding its biological function and calcium-binding mechanisms. To bridge this, the project utilized a Sequence-to-Structure workflow: predicting a high-quality tertiary structure via Homology Modelling, comparing it against known orthologs, and evaluating its binding affinity with the E-64 protease inhibitor using AutoDock Vina.

Overall Strategy & Logic

1

In-silico Profiling

Starting from the Primary Sequence (Q6SSJ2), physicochemical analysis (ProtParam) and secondary structure predictions (JPred/GOR IV) were conducted to assess protein stability and intrinsic folding tendencies prior to 3D modelling.

2

Tertiary Modelling

Homology Modelling (SWISS-MODEL) was employed to generate the 3D coordinates, followed by a rigorous stereochemical audit via SAVES v6.0 and MolProbity to ensure backbone geometry adhered to biological constraints.

3

Molecular Docking

The final phase involved simulating Receptor-Ligand interactions. Utilizing AutoDock Vina the E-64 inhibitor was docked into the predicted active site to determine binding affinities and visualize residue-level interactions.

Future Directions & Scalability

Molecular Dynamics (MD)

Transitioning from static docking to Dynamic Simulation using GROMACS or AMBER. This would allow for the observation of the NbDEK-E64 complex in a solvated environment, capturing conformational fluctuations and binding stability over time.

Next-Gen AI Integration

Leveraging AlphaFold 3 for end-to-end structure prediction and multimer modeling. Integrating deep learning scores would provide higher confidence in active site localization compared to traditional homology methods.
